## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a trusted, modern, and empathetic digital ecosystem tailored for university students navigating shared living spaces, hostel room selection, and roommate compatibility. The visual language blends collegiate reliability with contemporary lifestyle aesthetics, drawing inspiration from high-utility, modern travel and hospitality booking interfaces.

The emotional atmosphere is welcoming, organized, and serene. It eliminates the anxiety associated with dormitory allotments and interpersonal living arrangements through clear hierarchy, airy surfaces, and reassuring deep pine and forest tones. 

### Design Movement & Mood
- **Organic Modernism & Soft SaaS:** Clean, high-whitespace composition layered with soft tactile surfaces, curved organic boundaries, and subtle tinted ambient shadows.
- **Tone of Voice:** Friendly, transparent, structured, and student-first.
- **Visual Identity:** Crisp snow-white cards set on subtle pale mint-tinted canvases, balanced by deep forest teal primary elements that signal institutional authority and natural calm in the Himalayan foothills of Waknaghat.

## Colors

The color palette is rooted in tranquil forest greens, crisp alpine mint, and clear institutional balance. The colors ground the university experience with a mature yet fresh, collegiate appeal.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (`#005953`):** Deep Forest Pine. Used for primary CTAs, active tab icons, key navigation bars, and primary header texts.
- **Secondary (`#0A7A71`):** Luminous Teal. Used for interactive highlights, key metric badges, progress rings, and secondary action accents.
- **Tertiary (`#E07A5F`):** Terracotta Ember. Used selectively as an energetic accent for high-urgency notifications, roommate compatibility score highlights, and allocation deadlines.
- **Neutral Primary (`#12201E`):** Deep Spruce Charcoal. High-contrast typography base ensuring AA/AAA compliance across all display contexts.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base:** `#F4F9F8` — A delicate tinted mint-grey providing warmth and softness without clinical starkness.
- **Elevated Surfaces / Cards:** `#FFFFFF` — Crisp, pure white container cards providing strong structural distinction over the canvas.
- **Sub-surfaces & Inactive Chips:** `#E8F3F1` — Soft sage tint for badge backdrops, unselected category tabs, search fields, and disabled states.
- **Muted Typography:** `#5A7370` — Mid-tone slate green for secondary descriptors, timestamps, hostel rules, and metadata labels.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve a friendly, contemporary, and crisp mobile interface. Its geometric letterforms, rounded junctions, and clear counters maintain exceptional legibility across dense listings, questionnaire flows, and student ID badges.

### Typographic Principles
- **Title Hierarchy:** Large headlines feature tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) and bold weights to draw focal emphasis on student names and hostel blocks (e.g., Parmar, Shastri, Azad Bhavan).
- **Tabular & Metric Clarity:** Numbers in room allotments, compatibility percentages, and bed numbers employ medium-to-bold weights with proportional spacing to align cleanly with status pills.
- **Reading Comfort:** Body copy maintains a generous 1.4x to 1.5x line height ratio to ensure that personal habits, bio sections, and hostel guidelines remain effortless to digest on compact screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 4-column mobile fluid layout with a maximum container width of 480px on handheld screens, expanding gracefully to an 8-column layout on tablets and foldables.

### Rhythm & Grid Architecture
- **Screen Margins:** Standard horizontal gutters use `1.25rem` (20px) on mobile viewport widths, preventing edge crowding while preserving readable touch targets.
- **Vertical Flow:** Stacked cards and list blocks adhere to an 8pt spatial grid (`space-sm` for related internal items, `space-md` for grouped items within cards, and `space-lg` to separate distinct thematic modules).
- **Bottom Navigation Clearance:** Screens provide a dedicated `5.5rem` bottom spacer to account for the floating curved bottom navigation bar and active fab interactions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered surface luminance combined with ultra-soft, pine-tinted ambient drop shadows. This avoids harsh artificial blacks and creates a harmonious, tactile feel reminiscent of modern resort and travel cards.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** `#F4F9F8` baseline page surface. Zero shadow.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Pure white cards with an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(0, 89, 83, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(0, 89, 83, 0.03)`. Subtle 1px solid border using `rgba(0, 89, 83, 0.06)` for crispness against the light background.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Bottom Navigation):** Elevated navigation bars, floating action pills, and active filter overlays: `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -4px rgba(0, 89, 83, 0.12), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(0, 89, 83, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Allocation Overlays):** Bottom sheets and confirmation dialogs: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(18, 32, 30, 0.22)`, accompanied by a frosted glass backdrop filter blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over a 40% pine-tinted dark veil).

## Shapes

The design system incorporates generous, organic curvatures to communicate friendliness, approachability, and youthful cohesion.

### Shape Application Guidelines
- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Styled with a `1.25rem` to `1.5rem` (20px to 24px) corner radius. This provides structural definition without sharp points.
- **Input Fields & Search Bars:** Rounded at `1rem` (16px) for an ergonomic, inviting feel.
- **Pills, Badges, & Action Buttons:** Fully rounded (`9999px` / pill-shaped) to clearly signify clickability and filter functionality.
- **Student Avatars & Room Thumbnails:** Rounded rectangular frames at `1rem` (16px) or circular variants (`50%`) with a 2px white rim border.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Deep Forest Pine (`#005953`) background with pure white text, pill-shaped radius (`9999px`), minimum height of `52px`, `label-lg` typography. Features a soft forward arrow or relevant icon aligned right.
- **Secondary Button:** Light sage surface (`#E8F3F1`) with `#005953` text, no border, pill-shaped, designed for tertiary selections and secondary actions like "View Floor Map".
- **Floating Match Action:** Bold dual action button group (Decline / Request Roommate) featuring pill silhouettes with elevated Level 2 shadows.

### Chips & Status Pills
- **Match Compatibility Pill:** High-contrast pill with translucent background (`rgba(10, 122, 113, 0.12)`) and deep teal text (`#0A7A71`), displaying an icon and percentage score (e.g., `⚡ 94% Match`).
- **Hostel Allocation Badges:** Micro pills in `label-sm` with semantic state fills: 
  - *Allotted:* `#E1F5EC` background with `#0E6245` text.
  - *Pending Confirmation:* `#FFF3DC` background with `#8A5800` text.
  - *Vacant:* `#E8F3F1` background with `#005953` text.

### Cards
- **Student Profile Card:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` card with `20px` radius, housing a rounded avatar (64x64px), student major, semester tag, sleep schedule pill, and habit tags (e.g., "Early Riser", "Studious", "Non-Smoker").
- **Hostel Room Card:** Prominent photo header with rounded upper corners (`20px`), room type tag (e.g., "Double Occupancy - Geyser Included"), occupancy indicator, and instant "Request Roommate" CTA.

### Input Fields & Search
- **Search Bar:** Elevated or surface-inset pill-shaped container (`#FFFFFF` or `#E8F3F1`), housing a search icon, placeholder text in `#5A7370`, and a trailing circular filter button with high-contrast `#005953` accents.
- **Form Inputs:** Soft rectangular containers (`16px` radius) with interior labels, subtle borders (`rgba(0, 89, 83, 0.1)`), transitioning to a `2px` focus ring in `#0A7A71`.

### Bottom Navigation Bar
- **Floating Island Dock:** Suspended `16px` above the bottom screen margin, featuring a pure white card surface with Level 2 elevation, rounded at `28px`.
- **Navigation Items:** 4 to 5 primary destinations (Home, Matches, Rooms, Chat, Profile). Active tab displays a filled `#005953` icon with a micro teal dot indicator beneath; inactive tabs display `#5A7370` line glyphs.
